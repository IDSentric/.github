# Know who you're dealing with

IDSentric provides **identity and business verification** infrastructure with a developer-first API. Verify people and companies, issue reusable credentials, run background checks and screenings — all through a single integration.

## Products

| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| [Identity · KYC](https://www.idsentric.com/products/identity) | Verify a person in one look: phone-first document capture, face check (liveness, deepfake screening, face match), and explainable pass/review/declide in under two minutes. |
| [Business](https://www.idsentric.com/products/business) | Verify a company and its owners: company records, ownership graphs, UBO discovery, and AML — after an approved identity. |
| [Credentials](https://www.idsentric.com/products/credentials) | Turn an approved verification into lasting, reusable proof you can issue and anchor. |
| [Checks](https://www.idsentric.com/products/checks) | Ask a plain-language question — "is this person over 21?" — and learn only the answer. |
| [Screenings](https://www.idsentric.com/products/screenings) | SSN trace, criminal, sanctions, and more — background-screening data, after identity and business are verified. |
